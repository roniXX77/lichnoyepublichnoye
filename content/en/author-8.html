sometimes accidents just happen. for example, sometimes spring happens. a lot of people think it happens every year, but i know spring – it happens just once – single-use spring, accidental spring. i walk down the hall, see a door in front of me – on weak legs i go through it, there’s – another door, and behind it another door, and behind that – a door, and a door, and lotsandlotsandlots of doors. i write in circles, trying to tell you which door i saw today, without realizing – without trying to realize – that this door already slammed shut behind me once, and now i’m just staring blankly at the rusted handle of that very door from last spring. one day someone will tell me: «you wrote it very well. to write like that you have to be brave», and i’ll squeeze my eyes shut and answer – it’s an accident, one i was taught. someone once said to me: «let’s do crosswords in the park», and i squeezed my eyes shut and answered – let’s, let’s do crosswords in the park, it just so happens i’ve never done anything like that before, isn’t that nice? i once said: «you know, when i look at this moscow spring rain, i remember a song about him not coming and about rain in the region too. spring is crying like in an old movie» – it’s an accident behind a tiny tiny door. «sometimes accidents just happen», – the sign on the door answered me. i looked at the door and said to it: «i want a sign too, to know what this is, and to remember what sort of door i am and where i lead». i wanted to squeeze my eyes shut because there isn’t enough of me for even a little inanimate sign. and then someone kissed me on my hot forehead. someone answered me: «it’s an accident», and i hadn’t asked, i already knew that somehow a circular accident had happened again, the way spring happens year after year, the way hands first happen to touch, by accident the sign on my hot forehead repeated itself, and i didn’t even know that’s what it was called – repeated itself – that i could repeat myself – and that’s all i can do.
