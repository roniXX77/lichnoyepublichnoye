one, painfully accumulated and swollen – it happened after visiting the western gate, when i no longer had the strength to keep my hands off the blister full of liquid longing, when i was walking because of belgrade itself or the stamp in the passport, but because of something else, something foreign. when i lay down with my aching back on a hard air mattress in an empty apartment in los angeles, that blister burst and spilled in tears over the dirty carpet, like admitting i’d parted from the house i grew up in for a house of my own. i felt parted and ugly, but it wasn’t that very thing that came a little later with happiness. and along with happiness they delivered a couch, a fridge, a job, internet, string lights, drinking, nightmares, a coffee table, a mattress you don’t have to inflate, and as a gift – that very thing. a distinct loneliness, giving off laughter and light. when i heal my shell of the blisters of partings/notes/pointlessness, i discover something good that’s like a kiss and like childhood. but i also discover that i’m – a very lonely person here. smiling and shouting ceremoniously about my loneliness, the way i once shouted poems in serbian courtyards. today someone told me i was stupid if i didn’t stay an extra year in america. but is it stupid to love this america to tears and at the same time to know i still find myself alone here in a good way, and that means i’m already hoping for something familiar on the other shore? in the end i see two sides: how to heal burst blisters and how to rub them up fearlessly.
