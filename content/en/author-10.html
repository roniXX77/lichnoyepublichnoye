i like reading my girlfriends’ notes, thin as little strings. i want to touch the text lightly myself and join the tender chorus of girls’ voices. i don’t like that my fingers are growing rough from writing with the wrong thing and about the wrong thing, and so my once sweet text is dying off and taking on a weepy look. in russian they call this «drying up» – while i hold a little tit in my hands, even the heron flies south. i want to make a film. about childhood corked up in a bottle and thrown into the ocean. about rooms without curtains where it’s hard to fall asleep early in the morning. about a feeling like a bad pollen allergy in spring. about the funeral of the most important thing, which you don’t believe in even years later. about the color aquamarine and singing in a whisper. the problem with my film is it looks too much like my own situation. and i want to scream, run, dance. i want to make a film so i can call myself honest. but to be completely honest, i’m often a weak woman torn to pieces by my own screams and dancing. and when i think about film or text, i can’t help thinking about how the audience feels – i don’t want to hurt them. i really would like to show them the silly dreams i live in every day. but i won’t be able to take it much longer myself, and i don’t want the audience to realize they won’t be able to take it either. that one day all of us grow up. because somewhere on the other side there’s a girl who dreams of making films and works in museums. and she probably has beautiful brown eyes, and she even knows a thing or two about art, maybe she even writes poems and draws pictures in her spare time. i remember going to the buddhist temple in moscow for the first time and sitting on the floor in the middle – i sinned by hauling a passionate attachment along in my heart and sitting on the floor against the rules. i sin by thinking i’m special. and people around me sin by thinking the same thing about me. while that’s happening, i want to make a film. but one day i’ll make it and stop being special, because suddenly everybody will see how i feel. how i want to scream, run, dance. but how beautiful it is: to say out loud «i feel». i run. i am – dance. it hurts a lot. i lie often. sometimes i lie down on my bed and press my bare knees to my chin to feel all the dimming fragility of what’s happening. sometimes i fall into the abyss, and my cheeks turn aquamarine from lack of air. sometimes i look like a steinberg composition. but mostly i howl in outrage at how readily i flare up at the world around me. that would be the behind-the-scenes for cannes, and the film i’d make would be about warm dreams.
