absence - the defining quality of a person. i’m 19 and i live in los angeles. i’m absent from the narrow streets of chinatown, from my ex’s last school bell, from the cradle of my three little years, from the restaurant where my parents got married, from bed with strangers. that’s clear. a man sleeps in front of me on the train, his head pressed against the window – he’s on the train and on his way, but absent from both. my fuses have been knocked out – you can’t say that in russian – and i’m absent everywhere now. i ride the metro, trams, planes, i see houses and streets flying past, see us running to the coffee shop, kisses, fights, drunk friends, palms, tapestries in galleries – i’m absent. i’m no longer there. i’m not here.
