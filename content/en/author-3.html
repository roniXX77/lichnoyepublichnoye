It’s all slowly coming together in my head – it was recently established that the artist is an ideal Buddhist object with the properties of Sisyphus. This raises the question of how to feel reality by touch in artistic practice and accept its limits, and also why all Western art practices today are aimed at the conceptual and the political, i.e. at sustaining the illusion instead of accepting the idea that we determine materiality ourselves (for pretend). Waiting for Godot came to mind today and attached itself here, so it turns out the absence of reality has acquired a material form – waiting. In that case waiting can and should be the main artistic practice, waiting is linked to hope, which means to some sort of convincing yourself of your own reality again, which means to the Sisyphean labor of the artist. I remembered the diptych about the scuba diver and the extremely cute bride that I want to make in Los Angeles in June. The scuba diver – is waiting as a character. He came into my head when I was trying to pick suffering out of myself and turn it into a character. The ocean – is the world beyond the reality my character is waiting in. It’s the space outside the frame I want to become aware of. How will the second character help me see that frame? It hasn’t taken shape yet. But I like it. Right now life too feels more than usual like waiting and its absence, like an eternal waiting for Russia, but without the impulse inside to move in any particular direction. There’s a pleasant stirring inside me from these thoughts and words, as though they laid my own limits bare before me.
